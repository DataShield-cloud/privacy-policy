Hypnotix: Live TV IPTV Player ("Hypnotix", "the App", "we", "us"), published by DataShield-Studio, is a media player application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, how third-party advertising works inside it, and how Hypnotix relates to the content you choose to play through it. By downloading or using Hypnotix, you agree to the terms described below.

Overview

Hypnotix is a lightweight IPTV player — a tool for opening and playing media streams and playlists that you supply, such as M3U playlists or Xtream Codes logins. Hypnotix does not require you to create an account, and it does not ask for your name, email address, or phone number at any point.

The App currently has no backend server and no Firebase (or equivalent) analytics integration. Nothing you do inside the App is transmitted to servers operated by us, because we do not operate any.

Information we collect

Hypnotix does not collect, store, or transmit personal user accounts, email addresses, or phone numbers. We do not maintain any user database.

What stays on your device

Playlist URLs, Xtream Codes credentials, and any local media you add are stored only in the App's local storage on your device. This data is not sent to us and is not accessible to us.

What third parties may collect

The only data collection that occurs is carried out by Google's advertising and platform services (Google AdMob and Google Play Services), described in the next sections. We do not control the data these services collect beyond configuring which ad formats appear in the App.

Local storage permission

Hypnotix requests access to local device storage for one purpose only: to read, save, and play back playlist files (such as M3U files) and local media that you provide. This permission is not used to scan, upload, or share any other files on your device, and no file contents leave your device through this permission.

Your privacy rights (all regions)

Hypnotix is available worldwide, and this policy is written to meet the disclosure requirements of the regions below. Because the App collects no personal accounts and no data of its own, most of these rights concern the advertising identifiers handled by Google AdMob rather than data held by us.

European Economic Area & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are in the EEA or UK, our lawful basis for any advertising-related processing is your consent, collected through an in-app consent prompt where required. You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time through the App's consent settings or your device's ad settings.

California (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to request deletion, and to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (which, under California law, can include advertising identifiers used for personalized ads). You can exercise the opt-out through your device's ad settings or by contacting us below. We do not sell personal information for monetary payment.

Brazil (LGPD)

If you are in Brazil, you have the right to confirm whether processing occurs, request access, correction, anonymization, or deletion of your data, and to object to processing carried out on the basis of consent.

Canada (PIPEDA)

If you are in Canada, you have the right to access and request correction of any personal information held about you, and to withdraw consent to its collection, use, or disclosure, subject to legal restrictions.

Other regions

Wherever local law grants you equivalent rights — for example under Australia's Privacy Act, India's DPDP Act, or similar frameworks — we honor those rights to the same extent. To exercise any right described above, contact us using the details in the Contact us section; we will respond within the timeframe required by the applicable law.

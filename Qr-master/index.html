Overview

QR Master Toolkit ("we," "our," or "us") respects your absolute right to digital privacy. This application is designed as a fully standalone, offline utility for scanning and generating QR codes and barcodes.

✅ 100% Free Application
❌ Zero Advertisements
❌ No Data Collection
🌐 Operates Entirely Offline

1. Information Collection & Storage

We do not collect, store, transmit, process, share, or sell any personal data, usage telemetry, device identifiers, or scanned content under any circumstances.

  • No User Tracking: QR Master Toolkit does not include analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or user profiling tools.
  • Local Operation: All QR code generation, scanning, and processing take place locally in your device’s volatile memory (RAM). Scanned results never leave your device.

2. Device Permissions Explained

To provide full functionality, QR Master Toolkit requests minimal, essential system permissions. These are processed strictly on-device:

  • Camera Access (`CAMERA`): Required solely to capture live camera feed for scanning QR codes and barcodes. Camera frames are analyzed in real-time on your device and are never recorded, saved, or uploaded.
  • Storage / Gallery Access (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`): Required only when you explicitly select an image from your device gallery to scan a QR code or save a generated QR image to your device.

3. Advertising & Subscriptions

This application is completely free of charge with no monetization hurdles:

  • No Ads: QR Master Toolkit does not integrate Google AdMob or any third-party ad networks.
  • No Subscriptions / In-App Purchases: All features are unlocked and free forever. No payment credentials or account creations are ever required.

4. Global Regulatory Compliance

Because QR Master Toolkit operates entirely offline and collects zero user data, it naturally complies with all major global data privacy frameworks across all legal jurisdictions:

  • European Union (GDPR) & United Kingdom (UK GDPR): Since no personal data is collected or processed, no data controller or processor obligations are triggered under Article 6 of GDPR.
  • California, USA (CCPA / CPRA): We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as defined by California consumer privacy statutes.
  • India (DPDPA 2023): The app fully satisfies India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act requirements as no personal data is processed or transferred.
  • Brazil (LGPD) & International Privacy Laws: Fully compliant worldwide by architecture, owing to zero data retention.

5. Children’s Privacy

QR Master Toolkit does not address or target anyone under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because we do not collect any personal data whatsoever, the app is safe for users of all ages worldwide.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. However, our core philosophy of Zero Data Collection will remain unchanged. Any revisions will be published on this URL with an updated revision date.
